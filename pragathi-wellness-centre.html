About

A wellness centre built around natural healing and steady progress.

At Pragathi's Wellness Centre, we believe good health is created through consistent care, mindful movement, and therapies that support the body as a whole. Our centre brings together multiple healing approaches so every visit can feel practical, calming, and tailored to your needs.

Whether you are seeking relief, flexibility, recovery, stress reduction, or a more balanced lifestyle, our space is designed to support you with warmth and personal attention.

We combine yoga, therapy-led care, and natural wellness practices to help clients move better, feel better, and build healthier routines over time.
